Sakina Privacy Policy

Sakina Music Remover, a Chrome extension that removes background music from YouTube videos. Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Sakina collects no data. Everything it does happens on your own computer.

What Sakina does on your computer

  • While you watch a YouTube video, it processes the video's sound to remove the background music, using AI models that are included in the extension.
  • It saves your settings (switches and sliders) with Chrome's own settings storage.

What Sakina does not do

  • It does not send audio, browsing history or any other data to anyone, including the developer.
  • It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It runs only on youtube.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on Sakina's Chrome Web Store page.
